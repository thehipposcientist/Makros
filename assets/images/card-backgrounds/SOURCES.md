# Card Background Assets

These images are named by their app use so workout and meal card photos are easy to review in the asset folder.

Most images were downloaded from Pexels at `w=2200` with JPEG compression.

Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/
Wikimedia Commons files retain the license/public-domain status listed on their source pages.

## Pexels Rotation Images

`src/constants/cardBackgroundRotations.ts` adds deterministic Home-card
rotation variants using bundled compressed Pexels crops named
`pexels-card-{id}.jpg`. Source pages follow `https://www.pexels.com/photo/{id}/`.
